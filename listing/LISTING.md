## Description (Markdown, at least 150 characters)

A community addon for Polestar: every quest of the Drowned City, WoW Forever's sunken dungeon under the Vile Reef in Stranglethorn Vale, as one guide per faction.

- **Every quest in one route.** The Horde guide starts with Kin'weelay at Grom'gol, the Alliance guide with Sergeant Yohwa at the Rebel Camp, and both go on to the last hand-in.
- **Picks up where you are.** Steps you have already done are passed over.
- **One run.** Every quest that needs the dungeon is in your log before you go in, the idol quest included: the idol only drops while that quest is in your log.
- **Boss by boss.** Inside, the steps follow a recorded run: Zul'Alai, Var'Taka, Captain Dreadrise, the Deathless Guardians, Min'loth.

The guides are under **Community guides > The Drowned City Quests** in Polestar's guide menu (`/polestar guides`), for levels 35 to 40.

Polestar's own Dungeons window (`/polestar dungeons`) has a page for the Drowned City with a pick-up and a hand-in guide planned for your character. This addon gives you the same quests as one fixed route per faction, with the run in between. The two guides were first written for Polestar itself, which carries them in 0.4.1 under Miscellaneous; this addon is where they get their updates.

Every fact comes from the game's data, players' recordings and a run of my own, never from websites. Found a wrong step? Tell me in the discussion here.
