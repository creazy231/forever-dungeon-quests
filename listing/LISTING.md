## Description (Markdown, at least 150 characters)

A community addon for Polestar with quest guides for WoW Forever's dungeons. For each dungeon there is one guide per faction that takes every quest of it, from the first lead-in to the last hand-in, with the run itself boss by boss.

- **Every quest of its dungeon in one route**, in an order that walks you there once.
- **Picks up where you are.** Steps you have already done are passed over.
- **One run.** Every quest that needs the dungeon is in your log before you go in; a second only where a follow-up sends you back in.
- **Boss by boss.** Inside, the steps follow a recorded run where there is one, else the game's dungeon journal.

- **Wailing Caverns** (the Barrens, levels 17 to 24): Horde and Alliance. Tonga Runetotem's chain and Leaders of the Fang, Serpentbloom, Ratchet's and the druids' quests, one run (the Fanglords to Mutanus) and every hand-in.
- **Shadowfang Keep** (Silverpine Forest, levels 20 to 30): Horde, and Alliance Rogues and Warriors. The Book of Ur, Deathstalkers in Shadowfang, Arugal Must Die, the Rogues' Horn of Xelthos and the Warriors' Dragonslayer's Helm.
- **Blackfathom Deeps** (Ashenvale, levels 24 to 32): Horde and Alliance. Je'neu Sancrea's quests and the sapphires for the Horde; Gerrig, Darnassus' Thaelrid and pendants, and Gershala's brain stems for the Alliance; one run (Ghamoo-ra to Kelris) and every hand-in.
- **The Stockade** (Stormwind, levels 24 to 32): Alliance. Motley Garmason's Dark Iron War, Thelwater, Nikova, Berton and Millstipe, Bazil Thredd's chain after the Deadmines' letter, one run (Targorr to Bazil Thredd) and every hand-in.

- **Gnomeregan** (Dun Morogh, levels 29 to 38): Horde and Alliance. Rig Wars and Scooty for the Horde; Klockmort, Castpipe, Overspark, Ozzie, Shoni and Mekkatorque for the Alliance; Kernobee, the Sparklematic and the ring for both; one run (Grubbis to Thermaplugg) and every hand-in.
- **Razorfen Kraul** (the Barrens, levels 29 to 38): Horde and Alliance. Blueleaf Tubers, Willix, Charlga's quests (A Vengeful Fate, The Crone of the Kraul, An Unholy Alliance), Guano and Mortality Wanes, the Warriors' and Shamans' class chains, one run after a recorded one and every hand-in.
- **Scarlet Monastery** (Tirisfal Glades, levels 30 to 46): Horde and Alliance. - **The Drowned City** (Stranglethorn Vale, levels 35 to 40): Horde and Alliance. Kin'weelay's and Sergeant Yohwa's chains, Krazek's two quests, the idol, one run (Zul'Alai, Var'Taka, Captain Dreadrise, the Deathless Guardians, Min'loth) and every hand-in.

- **Maraudon** (Desolace, levels 46 to 55): Horde and Alliance. The Shen'dralas chain to Rot and Regret, the Pariah's Amulet of Union with the five Khans, Legends of Maraudon and the Scepter of Celebras, Twisted Evils, Vyletongue Corruption, Shadowshard Fragments, Corruption of Earth and Seed, Seed of Life, An'she's Radiance for the Horde, and every hand-in.

Brohann's chain through Rhapsody Shindigger for the Alliance, Fel'zerul's to the Fetishes of Hakkar for the Horde; Marvon's Stone Circle quests, Jammal'an the Prophet, Muigin's or Larion's haze, The Essence of Eranikus, The God Hakkar after Zul'Farrak, every class's Sunken Temple chain from its trainer, and every hand-in.
- **Blackrock Depths** (Blackrock Mountain, levels 52 to 60): Horde and Alliance, with the second run the princess's rescue needs. Magni's chain and Windsor's Jail Break! for the Alliance; Thrall's chain, the KILL ON SIGHT posters and Kargath's quests for the Horde; The Heart of the Mountain, A Taste of Flame, Dark Iron Legacy, Ribbly, The Love Potion, and every hand-in.
- **Blackmaw Hold** (Azshara, levels 55 to 60): Horde and Alliance. Beneath Nordrassil and the root caps, Blackmaw Magic, Ogtinc's claws and tusks, Ekkal's pouches, Gatekeeper Rageroar's Blackmaw Reclamation, the necklace, the Horde Paladins' Reliquary of Tyr, one run (Withermaw to the Unbound Spirit) and every hand-in.
- **Dire Maul** (Feralas, levels 56 to 60): Horde and Alliance, all three wings. Lethtendris's Web, Elven Legends, Pusillin, Shards of the Felvine, The Madness Within and the Shen'dralar treasure, Knot's quests and the Ogre Suit, the Taste Test, Unfinished Gordok Business, In Demand, Arcane Refreshment, the class books, and every hand-in.

- **Blackrock Spire** (Blackrock Mountain, levels 57 to 60): Horde and Alliance, the Lower Spire first, then both Spires with up to ten players. The Seal of Ascension quests, Kibler's and Ragged John's quests, Urok Doomhowl, Egg Freezing, the Eye of the Emberseer, Maxwell's and Drakkisath's quests for the Alliance, Warlord's Command to For The Horde! for the Horde, and every hand-in.
- **Stratholme** (Eastern Plaguelands, levels 58 to 60): Horde and Alliance, both sides and a second run. Tirion Fordring's chain to Of Love and Family, Betina's and the Duke's chains, Ezra Grimm, Houses of the Holy, The Restless Souls, Nathanos' chain to Ramstein for the Horde, and every hand-in.
- **Scholomance** (Caer Darrow, levels 58 to 60): Horde and Alliance. Eva Sarkhoff's chain from Krastinov to Kirtonos, Marduke's chain through Stratholme's Menethil's Gift to Ras Frostwhisper, the Barov Family Fortune and The Last Barov, Plagued Hatchlings, and every hand-in.
- **The Shaper's Terrace** (Un'Goro Crater, level 60): Horde and Alliance. Faranell's, Fuely's and Mayena's crystals and slimes for the Horde; Curator Thorius' ravasaurs and explorer's map for the Alliance; one run and every hand-in.

Every one of WoW Forever's new dungeons is in, and so is every classic dungeon from Ragefire Chasm to Scholomance.

The guides are under **Community guides > Forever Dungeon Quests** in Polestar's guide menu (`/polestar guides`). Polestar's own Dungeons window (`/polestar dungeons`) plans a pick-up and a hand-in guide for your character; this addon gives you a dungeon's quests as one fixed route instead. The Drowned City's guides were first written for Polestar itself, which carries them in 0.4.1 under Miscellaneous; this addon is where they get their updates.

The guides also read in Brazilian Portuguese, translated by soulrocha: a Portuguese (Brazil) client shows them in Portuguese, every other language as before.

Every fact comes from the game's data, players' recordings and runs of my own, never from websites. Found a wrong step? Tell me in the discussion here.
